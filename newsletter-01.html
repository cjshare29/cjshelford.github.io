WHERE WHISPERS GATHER NO1

Welcome to my very first newsletter!

Originally published September 2026
From the author
Where Whispers Gather

Well… here we are. My very first newsletter. If you've signed up, thank you. Seriously. It means a lot to me that you've decided to follow along with my journey of getting my very first novel out into the world. So, what have I been up to?

🎨 I've been designing a book cover

Over the last few weeks, I've probably spent far too much time designing book covers. I decided quite early on that I wanted to create the cover for Where Whispers Gather myself. I've deliberately stayed away from using AI for the actual cover, and instead I've been trying to learn as much as I can about cover design, typography, composition and what actually makes a cover work. Let's just say… there has been a lot of experimenting.

I've created quite a few different versions already, and I'm getting closer to something I'm really happy with. I might even share a couple of sneaky peeks of some of the designs I've been playing around with below. Don't get too excited though… They aren't the final cover. 😉

One of the cover designs shared in the original newsletter.

All that cover designing has, admittedly, taken a little time away from writing. But I'm back on track now, and the writing is moving again.

💷 Trying to publish my first book without spending a fortune

Another thing I've been thinking about a lot is the cost of actually publishing a book. This is my first novel, and I'm trying to see if I can get it published while spending as little as realistically possible. There are obviously costs involved, and I'm slowly figuring out what I actually need to spend money on and what I can learn to do myself. I might break down the costs and everything I've learned about publishing my first book in a future newsletter.

It could be interesting to see just how much it actually costs to get a book from a Pages document to something you can hold in your hands.

📖 To my potential beta readers…

If you signed up to potentially become a beta reader, thank you! I'm currently aiming for mid-to-late October to get the manuscript out to beta readers, so please keep an eye out for the sign-up forms. I also want to be completely upfront about something. Unfortunately, I won't be able to offer payment for beta reading. Please forgive me for that, but as I've mentioned, I'm trying to get my first book published while keeping the costs as low as possible.

At this stage, I'm relying on the goodwill and enthusiasm of you lovely readers rather than having the budget to pay people. In return, beta readers will receive a signed copy of the book once it launches as a thank you. And if you'd like one, I'm also more than happy to give you a little shout-out on my social media when the book is released. I'm incredibly grateful to anyone who is willing to give up their time to read my book and tell me what works, what doesn't, and where I can make it better.

✍️ And finally… the writing

This week's writing has been particularly exciting. Something happened that I honestly wasn't expecting. A character has managed to bring themselves back into the story. And the strangest thing? It happened completely naturally. I didn't force it. I wasn't sitting there thinking, How can I get this character back into the story? It just… happened. Even I was taken aback when I realised what I'd done. I'm not saying anything more about that, though. Spoilers. 😜 You'll just have to wait and see.

And that's it for my very first newsletter! Thank you for being here, for following along, and for taking an interest in Where Whispers Gather. I'm really excited to share more of the journey with you — the good bits, the frustrating bits, the things I get completely obsessed with (apparently book covers are one of them), and hopefully, eventually, a finished book.

Until next time,
C. J. Shelford
Author of Where Whispers Gather
