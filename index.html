Enter the story
The debut novel
Two men. Four realms. One dangerous love.

Zethan has spent his life serving inside a palace. Valerious is the prince he was never supposed to love. When the prince is taken, Zethan is drawn into a world of deadly trials, forbidden magic and secrets that could tear the realms apart.

In this world, their love isn't forbidden because they are two men. It is forbidden because one is a servant and the other is a prince.

Coming February 2027 · Currently preparing for beta readers in November 2026.

About the author
C. J. Shelford

I'm a UK-based writer and the author behind Where Whispers Gather, an adult M/M romantasy filled with forbidden love, magic, deadly trials and four magical realms.

I love creating immersive fantasy worlds where queer love is simply part of who the characters are, while the real danger comes from the world around them.

    Frequently asked questions
    What is Where Whispers Gather about?

    It is an adult M/M fantasy romance about Zethan, a palace servant, and Valerious, a prince. Their love crosses a divide of status and power, while deadly trials, forbidden magic and political secrets threaten their world.

    When will the book be released?

    February 2027 is the planned release date. I’ll share updates with newsletter subscribers as the publication plans progress.

    When will beta readers receive the manuscript?

    The manuscript is planned to go to beta readers in November 2026, before the next stage of editing.

    How can I vote on the potential book covers?

    Sign up to the newsletter using the form on this page. Subscribers will be invited to vote on the potential covers for Where Whispers Gather.

    What is The Whispered Realms?

    Where Whispers Gather is Book One of The Whispered Realms, a fantasy series set across four magical realms.

    Is the story about coming out or homophobia?

    No. The story takes place in a queer-normalised world. The central barrier to the couple’s relationship is their social position: one is a servant and the other is a prince.
