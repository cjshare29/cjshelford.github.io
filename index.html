Enter the story
The debut novel
Two men. Four realms. One dangerous love.

Zethan has spent his life serving inside a palace. Valerious is the prince he was never supposed to love. When the prince is taken, Zethan is drawn into a world of deadly trials, forbidden magic and secrets that could tear the Four Realms apart.

In this world, their love isn't forbidden because they are two men. It is forbidden because one is a servant and the other is a prince.

About the author
C. J. Shelford

I'm a UK-based writer and the author behind Where Whispers Gather, an adult M/M romantasy filled with forbidden love, magic, deadly trials and four magical realms.

I love creating immersive fantasy worlds where queer love is simply part of who the characters are, while the real danger comes from the world around them.
