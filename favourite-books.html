A little about my reading
Books I Love

I'm C. J. Shelford, an author of spicy M/M romantasy, and a lifelong lover of stories that combine magic, romance, adventure and characters who refuse to behave themselves.

I'm drawn to books with immersive worlds, complicated relationships, found family, forbidden love and plenty of emotional stakes. These are some of the books that have stayed with me, inspired me or simply given me the pleasure of getting completely lost in another world.
