The Characters
Zethan & Valerious

In a world shaped by magic, power and four ancient realms, a palace servant and a prince are brought together under circumstances neither of them could have imagined.

Get to know the two men at the heart of Where Whispers Gather.


The Servant
Zethan
Palace Servant · Autumn Realm

Zethan is a quietly intelligent, dryly humorous palace servant who has learned to survive by observing rather than demanding attention.

Polite on the surface and sharper underneath, he's remarkably good at reading people—especially nobles who mistake his position for stupidity.

He's curious, practical and stubborn, with a soft heart he doesn't advertise. He appreciates good food, simple comforts and the rare moments when nobody expects anything from him.

He isn't fearless; he's simply the sort of person who keeps going while frightened.

And beneath the ordinary servant everyone overlooks is a power capable of changing everything.

The Prince
Valerious
Prince · Summer Realm

Valerious has been raised within nobility, but he isn't simply defined by his title.

He carries himself with the confidence expected of a prince, yet there's more to him than the position he holds. He is accustomed to a world where people know who he is, where his decisions carry weight and where his existence is closely tied to the power of the Autumn court.

Then he is abducted.

Separated from everything familiar, Valerious is forced into a situation where his title can no longer protect him. His relationship with Zethan begins under extraordinary circumstances, and what develops between them challenges the boundaries between prince and servant.

There is still much about Valerious that remains hidden.

And perhaps that's exactly how he likes it.
