Chapter Eight

A Sneak peek

Quietly, Zethan tiptoed through the glasshouse in search of Valerious. One thing every good servant mastered was moving unseen, slipping through rooms without disturbing so much as a whisper. He paused, closing his eyes and holding his breath. Somewhere amongst the fragrant flowers and tangled vines, he hoped to catch the faintest sound of the prince. Instead, a gentle hand settled on his shoulder. Before he could turn, he already knew.

A warmth spread through him, dissolving every trace of uncertainty. He hadn't heard footsteps or sensed anyone nearby, yet he recognised the quiet calm that always seemed to accompany Valerious. It was as though the very air softened in his presence. As he turned, Valerious smiled. Without a word, they stepped into one another's embrace. For a moment, neither of them spoke. They simply looked into each other's eyes, the world beyond the glasshouse fading into silence. Zethan became aware of his own heartbeat, quick and unsteady, and somehow it seemed to fall into rhythm with Valerious's. Valerious lifted a hand and gently brushed it against Zethan's cheek. His fingertips traced the curve of his ear before resting lightly at the back of his neck. Zethan's hands found Valerious's waist, slipping beneath the edge of his blazer until his palms rested against the warmth of his bare skin. Drawn together by something neither of them could resist, the space between them slowly disappeared. Their lips met.

The kiss was tentative at first, filled with the uncertainty of two hearts discovering they beat as one. A shiver raced down Valerious's spine as Zethan's hand drifted along the gentle curve of his back. With a quiet urgency, Valerious guided them backwards until the wild vines climbing the glasshouse wall brushed against his shoulders. The kiss deepened, tender rather than hurried, each touch speaking the words they dared not say aloud. For one stolen moment, nothing else existed. But both of them knew the moment could not last. Reluctantly, they lingered in each other's embrace, knowing there would be a time for passion—just not tonight.
